A Bit About Me

I’m a 23-year-old Computer Science graduate from University College Dublin, originally from Longford, Ireland. I’m outgoing, adventurous, and love solving real-world problems with technology.

I studied Computer Science at University College Dublin (UCD), where I developed a strong foundation in software development, problem solving, and working with others on challenging projects. I’m particularly interested in cybersecurity, sustainability, and building software that can make a meaningful difference to people and the planet.

I enjoy working on projects where I can learn quickly, think creatively, and collaborate with others. Whether it’s designing secure systems, improving user experiences, or experimenting with new technologies, I’m always looking for opportunities to grow and contribute.

Active Hobbies

Outside of tech, staying active is a big part of who I am. These are some of the hobbies that keep me moving, motivated, and always looking for the next challenge.

Gaelic Football

Gaelic football has always been a huge part of my life. It keeps me fit, sharp, and focused on teamwork. Playing regularly has taught me discipline, resilience, and how to perform under pressure—skills that carry over into how I approach complex technical challenges.

Hiking

I love exploring Ireland’s trails and mountains. Hiking helps me reset, think clearly, and find inspiration away from the screen. It’s one of my favourite ways to disconnect, reflect, and come back to problems with a fresh perspective.

Surfing

Surfing challenges my balance, patience, and focus. Being out on the water is both energising and calming, and I enjoy the constant challenge of improving with every session—much like iterating on code or learning a new technology.

Snowboarding

I discovered snowboarding on winter trips abroad and instantly loved it. The mix of speed, control, and stunning mountain views makes it something I look forward to whenever I get the chance, and it reflects my love of learning new skills outside my comfort zone.
