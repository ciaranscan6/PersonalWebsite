A Bit About Me

I’m a 23-year-old Computer Science graduate from University College Dublin, originally from Longford, Ireland. I’m outgoing, adventurous, and love solving real-world problems with technology.

I studied Computer Science at University College Dublin (UCD), where I developed a strong foundation in software development, problem solving, and working with others on challenging projects. I’m particularly interested in cybersecurity, sustainability, and building software that can make a meaningful difference to people and the planet.

I enjoy working on projects where I can learn quickly, think creatively, and collaborate with others. Whether it’s designing secure systems, improving user experiences, or experimenting with new technologies, I’m always looking for opportunities to grow and contribute.
